FAQ

Questions, answered.

Who can join? Any prerequisites?

Grades 4 and above, zero prior knowledge. Advancement is by completion, not age.

How are classes scheduled?

1 session/week, 60 minutes. Level 1: 12 weeks, Level 2: 12 weeks, Level 3: 16 weeks. Flexible to school timetable.

Is coding required from day one?

No. Levels 1–2 are no-coding (electronics + logic ICs). Real Arduino C/C++ starts in Level 3.

Is the kit really free?

Yes — complete kit provided free with enrollment at each level (Level 1 kit ₹2,000+ value).

What does my child showcase?

Level 1: sensor robot, Level 2: wireless RC car, Level 3: TV-remote coded robot + automation — plus certificates & badges each level.
